### DP1: Feed Order
The public feed is ordered by Risk Status (High Risk first), followed by Recency. In a triage environment, the most dangerous, fast-spreading viral claims with multiple red flags must be routed to reviewers immediately before they cause real-world harm. 

### DP2: Visibility
Unverified claims are strictly held back and hidden from the public-facing view until they have been reviewed. If unverified claims were visible, this platform would inadvertently become a distribution vector for the exact misinformation it is trying to stop.

### DP3: Editing
Claims absolutely cannot be edited after submission. If the original text is altered, the automated risk flags (like >50% CAPS) and the reviewer's historical notes become invalid, destroying the integrity of the audit trail.
